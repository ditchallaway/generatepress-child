Questions

The things you wanted to ask.

If your question isn't here, our skincare team replies to every email within a day.

Is it safe for sensitive skin?

Yes — the formula is fragrance-free and pH-balanced. If you're new to vitamin C, start with the Gentle 5% strength every other morning and build up from there.

Can I use it with retinol?

Absolutely. We recommend Morning Glow in the AM and retinol at night. Using them at opposite ends of the day gives each ingredient the best chance to work without overwhelming your skin.

How long until I see results?

Most people see a brighter, more even tone within 2 weeks. Dark spots tend to fade visibly between weeks 4 and 8 with daily use.

What's the shelf life?

Sealed bottles last 24 months. Once opened, use within 6 months for peak potency. Store away from direct sunlight.

Will it pill under makeup?

No — the serum sinks in within 60 seconds. Wait a minute before layering moisturizer, SPF, or foundation and it stays invisible under everything.

Is it pregnancy-safe?

Yes. There are no retinoids, salicylic acid, or hydroquinone in the formula. As always, run any new product by your OB-GYN if you'd like extra peace of mind.
