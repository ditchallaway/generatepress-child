— Chapter I · The Movement

A movement composed of light.

The Aurore is built around our in-house Calibre 21A — a manufacture self-winding movement assembled by a single watchmaker over the course of four weeks.

Eighty-three components, each finished by hand. A 72-hour power reserve. A frequency of 28,800 vibrations per hour. Anglage polished on the dial side; perlage on the rear.

— Chapter II · The Case

38 mm of solid 18-karat gold.

The case is cut from a single ingot of 18k rose gold over six successive turning operations. Polished, satin-brushed, and polished again — each transition between finishes drawn by hand under a loupe.

The crystal is anti-reflective sapphire on both sides, lifted slightly proud of the bezel. Beneath the caseback: a second sapphire, revealing the calibre.

Each Aurore is hand-assembled to order, with a six-month delivery from the moment of reservation. Speak with a concierge for the current allocation list.

i

Dessiné à la main.

Every Aurore begins on a single sheet of vellum in our Le Locle atelier, where the dial geometry is drafted at 4× scale before being reduced and engraved.

ii

Assemblé à la pince.

The Calibre 21A is built up by a single watchmaker — eighty-three components, twenty-eight days, a microscope and a pair of brass tweezers.

iii

Fini à la loupe.

Each bevel is drawn by hand at 10× magnification. The perlage on the bridges is laid out one circle at a time. No two pieces are ever identical.
