QUESTIONS

Things people ask.

How does the fit run?

True to size with room for a flannel underneath. If you're between sizes and want to layer a wool sweater, go up one.

What's the difference between waxed and oilcloth?

Waxed canvas uses paraffin and natural waxes that solidify in the weave. Oilcloth uses linseed oil — heavier and prone to cracking. Ours is wax.

Can I re-wax it myself?

Yes. We sell a $24 re-wax kit in our accessories section. It's a Saturday-afternoon project — honestly satisfying once you've done one.

How do I clean it?

Cold water sponge-down for dirt. Never machine wash — the wax breaks down. For deep cleaning, send it to us with the re-wax service.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — to 32 countries via DHL Express. Duties calculated at checkout. Free shipping over $200 applies to international orders too.

What's your return policy?

60 days, no questions, full refund. Wear it, try it, see how it holds up. If it's not for you, send it back.
