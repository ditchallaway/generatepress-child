An overcoat worth keeping.

We started with a question: why does a coat that costs five times more last three times less? The Meridian is our answer.

Forty-eight panels, hand-pressed seams, a horsehair canvas chest-piece that breaks in with you over years. Buttons turned from buffalo horn in a small workshop in Naples.

The wool comes from a single flock outside L'Aquila. Undyed, washed in cold mountain water, woven on a 1920s shuttle loom that produces forty meters a day.
