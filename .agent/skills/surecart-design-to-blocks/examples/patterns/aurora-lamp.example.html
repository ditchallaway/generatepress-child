Description

Aurora is the desk lamp we wanted for ourselves — a single, beautifully made object that replaces three: your task light, your phone charger, and the cluttered little controller that runs them.

Inside the seamless aluminum housing sit 96 full-spectrum LEDs, calibrated to a CRI above 95 so the colors in front of you actually look like themselves. An ambient sensor reads the room a thousand times a second and quietly tunes brightness and color temperature to match the time of day.

The base hides an 18-watt USB-C charger that fast-charges most phones, earbuds, and accessories. Touch the brass ring once to power on, swipe to dim, hold to shift from warm reading light to crisp focus light. No remote, no app required — though both are there if you want them.

What does the 2-year warranty cover?

The Aurora is covered against manufacturing defects, electrical failure, and finish issues for two years from the date of purchase. Accidental damage is not covered, but we offer one free repair within the warranty window — just reach out and we'll send a return label.

How fast is shipping?

All US orders ship free via 2-day carbon-neutral courier. International orders ship via DHL Express and arrive in 4–7 business days. You'll get a tracking link the moment your lamp leaves the warehouse.

What is your return policy?

Try the Aurora for 30 days. If it doesn't earn its place on your desk, send it back in its original packaging for a full refund — including return shipping. No restocking fee, no questions asked.

Will it work with my smart home setup?

Aurora supports Apple HomeKit, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, and Matter out of the box. You can also integrate it directly with Home Assistant, IFTTT, and SmartThings via the official Aurora app.

How much power does it use?

At max brightness Aurora draws 18 watts — about half of a typical fluorescent task lamp. In Eco Mode the average daily draw is roughly 0.04 kWh, costing about $1.20 per year of use at average US electricity rates.
