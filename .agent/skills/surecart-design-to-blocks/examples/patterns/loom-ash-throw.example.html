FAQ

Questions, answered.

How is it woven?

On wooden handlooms outside Guimarães, Portugal. Each blanket is set up by hand: the warp threaded one strand at a time, the weft passed across by a single weaver. A full throw takes about nine hours on the loom plus another two days of hand-finishing.

What's the weight?

The Throw weighs ~1.4 kg (3 lbs); the Oversized ~2.1 kg (4.6 lbs); the Bed Runner ~1.1 kg (2.4 lbs). Heavy enough to drape, light enough to layer year-round.

Can I machine-wash?

Yes — cold water, gentle cycle, mild detergent. We recommend a mesh wash bag for the first three washes to protect the tassels. Don't tumble dry.

Will the colors fade?

Our dyes are low-impact and Oeko-Tex certified. Expect a gentle softening of saturation over the first year — most owners say this is the prettiest stage. Avoid direct, sustained sunlight.

How do I steam it?

A handheld steamer at medium heat, held 4–6 inches from the surface. Iron contact will flatten the weave; steam restores its loft.

What's the return process?

Use the prepaid label in your shipment to return any unwashed, unused blanket within 30 days for a full refund. International returns are paid by the customer.
