QUESTIONS

Things people ask.

How does it taste?

Light and green, with a soft hint of mint and matcha. Most customers describe it as the cleanest greens powder they've tried. It mixes well in cold water, oat milk, or a smoothie.

When will I feel results?

Most customers report noticeable changes in energy and digestion within 10 to 14 days of daily use. Full benefits accrue over four to six weeks as nutrient levels stabilize.

Is it organic?

Yes. Every ingredient is USDA-certified organic and non-GMO. Each batch is third-party tested for heavy metals, pesticides, and microbial safety, with results published on our site.

Can I take it with other supplements?

Yes — Atlas Greens pairs well with most daily supplements. If you take prescription medication, blood thinners, or are managing a thyroid condition, check with your doctor first.

How do I cancel my subscription?

From your account dashboard in two clicks, any time. No phone call, no retention script. You can also skip a month or change frequency without canceling.

Is it safe during pregnancy?

Atlas Greens is whole-food based, but it contains adaptogens and a high probiotic dose. We recommend consulting your OB-GYN before starting any new supplement during pregnancy or while nursing.
