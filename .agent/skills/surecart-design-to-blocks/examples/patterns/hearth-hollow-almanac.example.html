Common questions

Before you carry it home.

Which edition is right for me?

Standard is for occasional foragers — sixty-four entries, regional plates, the harvest calendar. Pro adds substitutions, kitchen notes, and seasonal recipes. Studio includes classroom-ready handouts and license for teaching.

How do downloads work?

After checkout you receive a linked PDF and ePub by email. Files are DRM-free and download as many times as you like. You can also re-download from any future order email.

Will I get future editions?

Yes — equinox refreshes ship to your inbox four times a year for as long as your edition is supported. Pro and Studio buyers also receive companion-volume early access.

Can I gift a copy?

Of course. Use the quantity stepper above and we will email each recipient a separately-addressed download link. License is per-reader; copies are not transferable after redemption.

Where do illustrations come from?

Every plate is hand-drawn at the print proof stage from specimens collected in season. Where possible we cite the field where the specimen was walked and the date of the drawing.
