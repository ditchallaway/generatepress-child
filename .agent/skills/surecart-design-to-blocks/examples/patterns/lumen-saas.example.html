QUESTIONS

Things engineers ask.

Does Lumen train on my codebase?

No. Lumen indexes your code so suggestions are context-aware, but your code is never used to train the underlying models. All inference happens on encrypted snapshots that are deleted within 24 hours.

Which models can I use?

Starter ships with GPT-4o-mini. Pro and Team unlock GPT-4o, Claude 4.7 Sonnet, Llama 3.3, and Mistral Large. You can switch per-repo or per-task.

How does the team plan billing work?

$39 per seat per month, billed annually. Add or remove seats anytime — we prorate. No usage caps or per-message fees.

What about offline / private model deployment?

Team plan customers can deploy a self-hosted inference layer or bring their own model endpoint. We're SOC 2 Type II and GDPR-compliant out of the box.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel any time from your dashboard. Annual plans are refunded prorata for the unused months — no questions asked.

Do you offer educational discounts?

Yes — 50% off Pro for students and faculty with a verified .edu email. Reach out to support@lumen.dev with proof of affiliation.
